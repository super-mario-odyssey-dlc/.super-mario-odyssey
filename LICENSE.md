## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions


<details>
<summary><strong>What is Super Mario Odyssey?</strong></summary>


Super Mario Odyssey is a 3D platformer game developed by Nintendo for the Nintendo Switch. It follows Mario on a globe-trotting adventure to rescue Princess Peach from Bowser using his new companion, Cappy.


</details>


<details>
<summary><strong>What platform is Super Mario Odyssey available on?</strong></summary>


Super Mario Odyssey is exclusively available on the **Nintendo Switch** platform.


</details>


<details>
<summary><strong>What is the difference between NSP and XCI formats?</strong></summary>


**NSP** (Nintendo Submission Package) is the digital eShop format, while **XCI** is the physical cartridge image format. Both contain the full game data.


</details>


<details>
<summary><strong>What type of game is Super Mario Odyssey?</strong></summary>


Super Mario Odyssey is a **3D platformer** with sandbox-style levels, exploration elements, and the unique Capture mechanic that allows Mario to control enemies and objects.


</details>


<details>
<summary><strong>How many kingdoms are in Super Mario Odyssey?</strong></summary>


Super Mario Odyssey features **over 15 unique kingdoms** to explore, each with its own theme, challenges, and collectibles.


</details>
